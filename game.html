♠ GAME GUIDE

SPADES • RULES & HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Object of the Game

Win tricks by playing the strongest valid card. In Spade Trump, Spades are always the trump suit. The team that earns the required tricks gets the score.

👥 Teams

In the 6-player game there are two teams of three players. Players 1, 3 and 5 form the Blue Team. Players 2, 4 and 6 form the Red Team.

📢 Bid

Before playing, each player selects a bid. The available bid values are 0 through 7. Your bid is shown below your player seat.

🃏 Playing Cards

Select one card from your hand and press PLAY CARD. Follow the suit that was led whenever possible. A Spade can win a trick when Spades are trump.

♠ Trump

Spades are the permanent trump suit in the Spade Trump game. A strong Spade can beat cards from other suits.

🏆 Score

The scoreboard shows the current score for both teams. The goal is to complete the required tricks and finish the game with the highest score.

⏱️ Turn

Wait for your turn, choose a valid card from your hand and play it. The selected card is raised so you can clearly see which card will be played.
