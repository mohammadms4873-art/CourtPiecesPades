6 PLAYER GUIDE

COMPLETE SPADE TRUMP RULES

♠ Spade Trump — 6 Players

A team card game for six players. Players are divided into two teams of three. Spades are always the permanent trump suit.

RULE 01

Players & Teams

  • There are exactly 6 players.
  • Player 1, Player 3 and Player 5 form Team Blue.
  • Player 2, Player 4 and Player 6 form Team Red.
  • The two teams compete against each other.
RULE 02

Cards

The game uses a standard 52-card deck together with two special Jokers. Each player receives 9 cards.

Every player must play all 9 cards during the complete game sequence.
RULE 03

Permanent Trump

Spades are always the trump suit. A valid Spade can normally defeat cards from another suit according to the rules of the current trick.

RULE 04

Team Declaration

Before play begins, players announce how many tricks they expect their side to win.

  • The declarations of the three teammates are combined for the team total.
  • Team minimum declaration: 2.
  • Maximum declaration: 7.
  • Players may change their declaration during the allowed declaration window.
RULE 05

Example Declaration

If Blue Team players declare 1, 2 and 1, their combined team declaration is 4.

Blue Team = 1 + 2 + 1 = 4 tricks

The final team score is determined by comparing the declared amount with the actual number of tricks won.

RULE 06

Card Strength

The special card order in Spade Trump places the two Jokers into the game's special hierarchy.

1. Red Joker
2. Ace of Spades
3. Black Joker
4. King of Spades

Other cards follow the normal strength order for their suit.

RULE 07

Joker Rules

  • Jokers may be used during a trick when the game rules permit them.
  • A Joker cannot normally be used to lead a trick.
  • Red Joker is stronger than Ace of Spades.
  • Black Joker is below Ace of Spades in the special hierarchy.
  • If a player is forced to lead with a Joker as the final card, the special loss rule applies.
RULE 08

Following Suit

When a player leads a suit, players should follow that suit when they have a valid card of the required suit.

When a player cannot follow the required suit, other legal cards may be played according to the game's trump and Joker rules.

RULE 09

Hearts / Special Play

When the special Hearts situation occurs, the Joker rules remain active. A player may use a permitted Joker to take the trick under the applicable rule.

A Joker can take a legal trick in situations where a Spade cannot win under the special Hearts rule.
RULE 10

Six Starting Players

The starting player changes after each 9-card sequence.

Sequence 1 → Player 1
Sequence 2 → Player 2
Sequence 3 → Player 3
Sequence 4 → Player 4
Sequence 5 → Player 5
Sequence 6 → Player 6
RULE 11

Nine Cards

Each player receives nine cards. The complete sequence is played until all nine cards have been used.

After the nine cards are completed, the result is recorded and the next distribution begins.
RULE 12

Player Timer

Each move has a limited time. The standard game timer is 10 seconds for a player's move.

If the player times out, the player can be removed from the active table and replaced by a Bot.
RULE 13

Scoring

Team scoring is based on the relationship between the team's declaration and the number of tricks actually won.

Example:
Declaration = 4
Tricks Won = 4
Result = +40

If the team fails to achieve its declaration, the corresponding negative score is applied.

RULE 14

Seven Declaration

A declaration of seven is a high-risk declaration.

Successful 7 declaration = +140 points.
Failed 7 declaration = -140 points.
RULE 15

End of Game

After all six complete sequences have finished, the scores of both teams are calculated.

The team with the higher final score wins the game.

4 PLAYER GUIDE

COMPLETE CLASSIC SPADES RULES

♣ Classic Spades — 4 Players

A traditional four-player Spades game played as two teams of two players. Spades are the permanent trump suit.

RULE 01

Players & Teams

  • Exactly 4 players participate.
  • The players are divided into two teams.
  • Each team contains two players.
  • Teammates work together to reach their combined objective.
RULE 02

Deck & Distribution

The standard 52-card deck is used. Cards are distributed evenly between the four players.

Each player receives 13 cards.
RULE 03

Spades Are Trump

Spades are always the trump suit. A Spade can beat a card from another suit when legally played.

RULE 04

Bidding

Before the play begins, players estimate how many tricks they expect to win. The bids of teammates determine the team's target.

RULE 05

Playing a Trick

  • One player leads the trick.
  • Other players follow the led suit when possible.
  • If a player cannot follow suit, another legal card may be played.
  • A Spade can trump another suit.
  • The strongest legal card wins the trick.
RULE 06

Winning Tricks

The player who wins a trick normally leads the next trick.

Teammates should coordinate their cards and declarations to achieve their target.

RULE 07

Scoring

The final score is based on the team's declared target and the number of tricks actually won.

Meet the target → positive score
Miss the target → penalty according to the selected scoring rules
RULE 08

Time Limit

Every move has a limited time. Players should make their move before the timer expires.

Timeout may cause the player to be replaced by an automated Bot.
RULE 09

Team Strategy

Watch the cards already played, estimate remaining Spades and manage your bid carefully.

  • Do not overestimate your tricks.
  • Track high Spades.
  • Protect your teammate when possible.
  • Use trump cards strategically.
RULE 10

Game Result

When the complete hand is finished, team scores are calculated and the team with the higher final score wins.

2 PLAYER GUIDE

COMPLETE TWO PLAYER RULES

🃏 Two Player Spades

A fast head-to-head card game designed specifically for two players. Spades remain the permanent trump suit.

RULE 01

Players

The table contains exactly two active players. Each player competes directly against the other.

RULE 02

Cards

The game uses a standard card deck. Cards are distributed between the two players according to the active game configuration.

RULE 03

Trump Suit

Spades are the permanent trump suit. A legal Spade can defeat a card from another suit.

RULE 04

Starting the Trick

One player starts the trick by playing a legal card. The opponent then responds according to the suit-following rules.

RULE 05

Following Suit

  • Follow the led suit whenever possible.
  • If you cannot follow suit, a legal alternative may be played.
  • Spades can trump another suit.
  • The strongest legal card wins the trick.
RULE 06

Trick Winner

The player who wins the trick receives the trick and normally leads the next one.

RULE 07

Scoring

The scoring system compares the player's objective with the tricks actually won. Successful play produces positive points, while failing the required objective can result in a penalty.

RULE 08

Time Limit

Each player has a limited amount of time to make a move.

If the timer expires, the automated Bot can take over the player's position.
RULE 09

Strategy

  • Remember which high cards have already been played.
  • Track the remaining Spades.
  • Avoid wasting powerful trump cards.
  • Plan your next trick before playing.
