Tokens

Every new player starts with 100 Tokens. Each game entry costs 50 Tokens.

Video Reward

Before entering a game, you must watch the required YouTube video. Completing the video gives you 25 Tokens.

Video Progress

Each game has its own video list. Your progress is saved separately for every game. Your first entry shows Video 1, your second entry shows Video 2, and so on.
