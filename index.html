♠ SPADE TRUMP

6 PLAYERS • 3 VS 3
GUIDE IMAGE 01
SPADE TRUMP RULES

Replace this area with your final 9:16 game rules image.
PLAYERS & TEAMS
6 players play at one table.
Players 1, 3 and 5 = Team Blue.
Players 2, 4 and 6 = Team Red.
Teammates play together as a team.
CARDS
52 standard cards + 2 Jokers are used.
Each player receives 9 cards.
All 9 cards must be played.
The game contains 6 rounds.
TRUMP
♠ Spades are always Trump.
SPECIAL CARD ORDER
Red Joker → Ace of Spades → Black Joker → King of Spades.
Jokers may be used during play but cannot normally lead a trick.
BIDDING
Players announce the number of tricks they expect to take.
The three teammates' bids are combined.
Minimum Team Bid: 2.
Maximum Team Bid: 7.
PLAYING TRICKS
The starting player leads the trick.
Players play one card in turn.
The strongest valid card wins the trick.
Spades can win as Trump.
STARTING PLAYER
Round 1 → Player 1
Round 2 → Player 2
Round 3 → Player 3
Round 4 → Player 4
Round 5 → Player 5
Round 6 → Player 6
SCORING
Bid 4 + Win 4 = +40 POINTS.
Bid 7 + Win 7 = +140 POINTS.
Bid 7 + Fail = -140 POINTS.
TIME LIMIT
10 seconds per move.
If a player leaves or times out, a Bot takes over the player's position.

♣ CLASSIC

4 PLAYERS • 2 VS 2
GUIDE IMAGE 02
CLASSIC RULES

Replace this area with your final 9:16 game rules image.
PLAYERS & TEAMS
4 players play at one table.
Players form 2 teams of 2.
Teammates work together to win tricks.
CARDS
A standard 52-card deck is used.
Cards are dealt between the 4 players.
TRUMP
The Trump suit is selected according to the Classic rules.
Trump cards have priority over non-Trump cards.
PLAYING TRICKS
One player leads the trick.
Players follow the required suit when possible.
The strongest valid card wins the trick.
The winner leads the next trick.
TEAM PLAY
You play together with your teammate.
Winning tricks helps your team.
SCORING
Scores are calculated using the Classic scoring system.
TIME LIMIT
Each move has a limited time.
If a player times out, Bot assistance can continue the game.

♠ SPADE DUEL

2 PLAYERS • 1 VS 1 • 200 POINTS
GUIDE IMAGE 03
SPADE DUEL RULES

Replace this area with your final 9:16 game rules image.
PLAYERS
2 players compete directly.
There are no teams.
CARDS
Cards are dealt according to the Duel rules.
Players play their cards trick by trick.
TRUMP
♠ Spades are always Trump.
PLAYING TRICKS
Players take turns playing cards.
The strongest valid card wins the trick.
200 POINT SYSTEM
SPADE DUEL uses the 200-point system.
Players collect points during the game.
The game is played toward the 200-point target.
WINNER
The player who reaches the 200-point winning condition wins the Duel.
